What the data is saying

Three structural reads on the region's economies.

FINDING 01

Headline growth hides the real story

Resource booms can lift headline GDP far above peers while the broader economy barely moves. Incumbent producers with decades of extraction show near-zero growth — a credible forward signal.

FINDING 02

The enclave problem, in numbers

Headline GDP can exceed 60% in a year while the non-oil economy grows low single digits. Agriculture, manufacturing, and services often see no proportional spillover. The canonical enclave effect.

FINDING 03

Revenue discipline is the dividing line

When commodity revenue is monetized rather than sterilized, inflation runs away — Suriname exceeded 59% in 2021. Sovereign funds exist to avoid exactly this, if discipline holds.
